The catalogue can be licensed for creative and commercial use, and sound environments can be built for organizations directly. Both come with written terms and a documented production record.

Licensing

Five kinds of use.

02

Beds, intros and transitions built to sit under speech without fighting it.

03

05

Brands

Sound identity and campaign use, including exclusivity where the brief needs it.

Every licence

What you get in writing

  • Permitted media and platforms
  • Territory and duration
  • Exclusivity, where agreed
  • Permitted edits
  • The production record behind the track

Why the paperwork matters here. Distributors and platforms increasingly ask for proof of commercial rights on AI-assisted audio. Every track carries a written record of how it was made and under what terms — so a licence from me holds up when someone asks.

For organizations
