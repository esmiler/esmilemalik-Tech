Home / Sound Journeys

Five journeys, five moments.

Each journey is built for one hour of one kind of day. Below each is the specification it was made against — pacing, opening, ending — and the production prompt itself, published in full.

Journey 01 · Morning Grounding Journey

Morning Calm

For beginning the day without rushing into it.

The first hour of a day sets its pace. This journey is made for the person who wants to be awake before they are busy — unhurried openings, light that arrives gradually, nothing that demands a decision. Designed to be put on before the first message is read.

Pacing
Very slow
Opening
Near-silence, single sustained tone entering under the threshold
Ending
Gently forward-leaning
Pricing
On request
The production prompt 
Create an AI-assisted long-form ambient soundscape for the first waking hour of the
day, before work begins.
Emotional intention: unhurried arrival; awake but not yet busy.
Tempo: very slow.
Sound palette: warm felt piano, low sustained strings, soft room tone,
distant birdsong, gentle analogue tape hiss.
Arrangement: gentle arrival → subtle variation → soft, non-dramatic resolution
that leans very slightly forward.
Production: warm, spacious, uncluttered, smooth dynamics, no harsh peaks.
Avoid: sudden drops, aggressive percussion, disturbing sounds, excessive
       vocals, melodrama, abrupt ending, anything that reads as an alarm.
Journey 02 · Focus Journey

Peaceful Focus

For reading, coding, writing, studying — quiet work that needs company, not distraction.

The hardest brief of the five, because the music has to be genuinely present and genuinely ignorable at the same time. Built with low event density and no sudden arrivals: nothing that pulls your eyes off the page, nothing so empty that the room starts to feel loud.

Pacing
Steady, unvarying
Opening
Already in motion — no announced beginning to notice
Ending
Unobtrusive, loopable
Pricing
On request
The production prompt 
Create an AI-assisted long-form ambient soundscape for sustained quiet work —
reading, writing, studying or coding.
Emotional intention: steady company that never asks for attention.
Tempo: slow, entirely unvarying.
Sound palette: soft synth pads, muted rhodes, very low bass drone,
faint vinyl texture.
Arrangement: no discernible beginning → minimal variation held for long
stretches → ending that could loop unnoticed.
Production: warm, spacious, uncluttered, smooth dynamics, no harsh peaks,
low event density throughout.
Avoid: sudden drops, aggressive percussion, melodic hooks, key changes,
       vocals of any kind, anything that marks the passage of time.
Journey 03 · Reflection Journey

Quiet Reflection

For journalling, prayer, contemplation, gratitude.

Made to hold space rather than fill it. More silence than the others, wider intervals, and a deliberate restraint about resolution — the music does not tell you how to feel about what you are sitting with. It stays alongside you while you work that out.

Pacing
Very slow, with real silence between phrases
Opening
A single note, then space
Ending
Open and unhurried — deliberately unresolved
Pricing
On request
The production prompt 
Create an AI-assisted long-form ambient soundscape for journalling, prayer or
quiet contemplation.
Emotional intention: spacious company that does not direct the feeling.
Tempo: very slow.
Sound palette: sparse prepared piano, bowed glass, low choral pad
without words, deep room reverb.
Arrangement: single sustained entry → long phrases separated by genuine
silence → ending left open rather than resolved.
Production: warm, spacious, uncluttered, smooth dynamics, no harsh peaks;
allow real silence — do not fill every bar.
Avoid: sudden drops, percussion of any kind, resolving cadences, uplifting
       or consoling gestures, vocals, melodrama, abrupt ending.
Journey 04 · Evening Decompression Journey

Evening Release

For the crossing from a working day into rest.

The most explicitly transitional of the five: it starts closer to where a long day leaves you and travels somewhere quieter. Tension is acknowledged in the opening rather than ignored, then given room to unwind across the whole piece.

Pacing
Slowing throughout — ends slower than it begins
Opening
Denser and slightly unsettled, meeting the day where it left you
Ending
Settled and soft
Pricing
On request
The production prompt 
Create an AI-assisted long-form ambient soundscape for the transition out of a
demanding working day into rest.
Emotional intention: tension acknowledged, then gradually released.
Tempo: slow, decelerating across the piece.
Sound palette: warm analogue synth, muted cello, soft rain texture,
low pulsing drone that thins out over time.
Arrangement: slightly dense and unsettled opening → progressive thinning
and slowing → soft, settled resolution.
Production: warm, spacious, uncluttered, smooth dynamics, no harsh peaks.
Avoid: sudden drops, aggressive percussion, disturbing sounds, excessive
       vocals, melodrama, abrupt ending, any late reintroduction of energy.
Journey 05 · Emotional Reset Journey

Light Within

For recovering perspective, and reconnecting with hope.

The warmest journey, and the only one that consciously lifts. Made for the hour after something difficult, when what is needed is not distraction but a reminder that the difficulty is not the whole picture. It ends with more light than it started with.

Pacing
Slow, gradually opening
Opening
Low and close, then widening
Ending
Warm and resolved — brighter than the opening
Pricing
On request
The production prompt 
Create an AI-assisted long-form ambient soundscape for the hour after something
difficult, when perspective is returning.
Emotional intention: quiet hope, arriving slowly and without insistence.
Tempo: slow.
Sound palette: warm strings, soft brass swell, felt piano,
gentle harp figures, wide bright reverb.
Arrangement: low and close opening → gradual widening and brightening
→ warm, fully resolved ending.
Production: warm, spacious, uncluttered, smooth dynamics, no harsh peaks;
the lift must feel earned, never triumphant.
Avoid: sudden drops, aggressive percussion, anthemic climaxes, melodrama,
       excessive vocals, sentimentality, abrupt ending.

A note on language

What these are designed to do.

These journeys are designed to support calm, create a peaceful atmosphere, and accompany reflection. They are not therapy, not medical treatment, and make no claim to cure, heal, or treat any condition. Any future wellbeing claim will arrive only with qualified clinicians standing behind it — and it will say so plainly when it does.
