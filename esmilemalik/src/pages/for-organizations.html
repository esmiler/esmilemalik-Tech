Who this is for

Three kinds of organization.

The constraints differ — procurement, margin, mission — so the builds differ. The sequence does not.

Government

Fixed headcount, rising volume, and a compliance surface that makes most off-the-shelf AI unusable.

  • Citizen enquiry and service assistants
  • Records, forms and document workflows
  • Internal process automation
  • Compliance-aware model and hosting choices

Private & Business

Growth capped by manual process — support queues, onboarding, back-office work that scales only by hiring.

  • Customer-service and sales agents
  • Back-office and operations automation
  • AI strategy and tooling decisions
  • Team enablement and internal standards

NGO & Non-Profit

Small teams, restricted funds, and reporting obligations that consume the time meant for the mission.

  • Reporting and grant-writing support
  • Field-data intake and summarization
  • Beneficiary communication tooling
  • Deliberately low running costs

Discipline

What is committed to in writing.

Written scope of work, always

Every engagement, at every size, carries a written SOW covering deliverables, the LLM and platform being used, timeline, and IP-transfer terms. Nothing starts on a handshake.

Compliance before publicity

Government and NGO builds require documented compliance review and written sign-off before the organization is ever named publicly — including on this site.

Data protection by design

Where a build touches personal or health data, the data path is designed against applicable law — Nigeria’s NDPR and any sector rules that apply — as part of the architecture, not as a later patch.

No testimonial without permission

Nothing you say about the work appears anywhere without your written permission first. That rule has no exceptions and no expiry.
