The method

A³ — Audit, Architect, Automate.

Most AI projects fail at step one, because nobody measured the process before automating it. This runs in the same order every time, whether the job is a single prompt pack or an organization-wide platform.

01 · Audit

Find where the hours go

Map the real process as it is performed, not as it is documented. Identify what repeats, what waits, and what only a human should ever touch. Nothing gets built before this is on paper.

02 · Architect

Design the system

Decide what AI handles, what stays manual, and where the handoffs sit. Choose the model layer, the data path, and the failure behaviour — before a line of it is committed to.

03 · Automate

Build, test, hand over

Ship the prompts, workflows or platform, test them against real inputs, and hand over documentation your team can run without me in the room.

At micro scale

One vertical — a fashion campaign workflow, a clinic’s intake paperwork. Audit the task, architect the prompt chain, automate the repeatable part. Days, not quarters.

At macro scale

A whole organization — an agency’s citizen enquiry line, a company’s support function. Same three moves, applied across departments, with governance and monitoring attached.
