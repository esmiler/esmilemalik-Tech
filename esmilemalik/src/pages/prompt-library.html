The five verticals

Where the packs live.

Four named industries and one open door. The fifth exists because the method is domain-independent — the industries above are simply where the demand showed up first.

Vertical 01

Fashion

conceptmood boardcampaign copy

The failure mode in fashion work is drift: the fifth image no longer belongs to the same collection as the first. These prompts hold a defined aesthetic across an entire body of work — silhouette language, palette discipline, fabric vocabulary and styling logic fixed up front, so variation happens inside the concept rather than escaping it.

Covers concept and silhouette exploration, mood-board direction, lookbook and campaign copy, and season-to-season continuity when a line has to feel like a follow-up rather than a fresh start.

Vertical 02

Medicine

administrativeeducationalresearchpatient comms

Clinical teams lose enormous time to everything that is not clinical: documentation, referral letters, teaching material, literature review, and explaining the same condition in plain language forty times a month. That is the entire scope here.

Covers administrative and documentation drafting, educational and training material, research and literature summarization, and patient-facing plain-language explainers that a clinician reviews before it reaches anybody.

Scope limit, stated plainly. Nothing in this vertical offers diagnostic or treatment guidance, and nothing is designed to sit between a clinician and a clinical decision. Deployments involving patient data are built to respect applicable data-protection law, including Nigeria’s NDPR, and a qualified professional reviews any output that reaches a patient.

Vertical 03

Music

compositionlyricssound design

Built for producers and writers who want a faster route out of a blank session, not a machine that finishes the track for them. The prompts are deliberately generative rather than conclusive — they produce options to react against.

Covers arrangement and progression sketching, lyric drafting and rewriting with a held voice, sound-design and texture direction, and reference translation — turning “make it feel like that record” into instructions a tool can actually use.

Vertical 04

Arts, Graphics & Animation

style lockcharacter consistencysequencing

Two things break first when visual work is generated at volume: style consistency and sequence continuity. Both are engineering problems, and both are what this pack is for.

Covers locked visual-style systems that survive across a full set, character and asset consistency between frames, animation and shot sequencing, and the handoff notes a designer or animator needs to take the output into production tooling.

Vertical 05
